Whisker Hollow: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026. Applies to Whisker Hollow: Cozy Cat Cafe, version 1.7 and later, on Android.

Whisker Hollow ("the game") is a single-player game made by AanyaLabs ("we"). You can play it offline. We built it to collect as little as possible. This page explains what happens to your information and the choices you have.

Contact: SUPPORT_EMAIL

1. The short version

  • There are no accounts, no logins and no chat. We never ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos.
  • Your progress is saved on your device.
  • We do not collect any personal data, and nothing about your play is sent to us.
  • The game has no ads and no in-app purchases in this version.

2. Information stored on your device

WhatWhyWhere it goes
Game progress: cafe name, an optional name you give your player, cats, coins, decor, settingsSo you can keep playingSaved only in the app's storage on your device
Gameplay statistics (for example "a cafe day finished", "a recipe cooked")To balance the game during testingKept on your device in a capped log. Not sent to us.
Cat photos you take in Photo ModeSo you can save or share themStay on your device. They leave it only if you choose Share, through your phone's own share sheet. We never receive them.
Local reminders, if you switch them on"The tip jar is full", "A new day in Whisker Hollow"Scheduled by your phone. They are not push messages and are not sent from a server.

Android may include the game's saved data in your personal Google device backup, which Google handles under its own privacy policy. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

3. Information that leaves your device

None, in this version, other than what you choose to share yourself (for example a photo you send through the share sheet). The game does not include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services. Google Play itself may collect usage and crash data under Google's own policies.

If a future version adds purchases, ads or analytics, we will update this page and the Google Play "Data safety" section before that version is released.

4. What we do not do

We do not sell personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or build profiles. We do not share information with third parties, because we do not collect any.

5. Children

Whisker Hollow is designed for a general audience aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Because the game itself asks for no personal information, a younger player cannot give us any.

6. How long we keep data

Data on your device stays until you reset progress or uninstall the game. We hold no data about you.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your game data: Settings, then Reset progress, or uninstall the game.
  • Notifications: turn them off in the game's Settings or in your phone's settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or US state laws such as the CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal information. Because we do not hold any, there is nothing for us to provide, but you can email us any question and we will reply within 30 days.

8. Changes

If the game starts handling data in a new way, we will update this page before that version is released and change the date above.
